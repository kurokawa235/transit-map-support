Frequently Asked Questions

Which cities are supported?

Transit Map covers 78+ cities across Asia, Europe, North America, South America, Oceania, the Middle East, and Africa. Open the city selector in the app to browse the full list.

How do I use the app offline?

In the city selector, tap the download icon next to a city to save its map tiles and transit data for offline use. Downloaded cities are marked as "Saved Offline."

Can I search for routes between stations?

Yes. Tap the search icon on the map screen, then select an origin and destination station. The app will find connecting routes including transfers.

Why is a city shown as unavailable?

Some cities may be temporarily unavailable if transit data is being updated. Try again later, or download the city for offline access when it becomes available.

How do I delete offline data?

You can delete the app to remove all cached and offline data. Alternatively, go to your device's Settings > General > iPhone Storage > Transit Map to manage storage.
